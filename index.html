Who voted how: exit polls

Edison Research exit poll results for Florida, as published by CNN. Each row shows how a group split between the two main candidates and how much of the electorate it made up.

How this data is handled

  • Weighted: Yes. These are the published crosstabs, which the pollster has already weighted. The 2020 total row (Biden 47.86, Trump 51.22) matches the actual Florida result. I did not re-weight anything.
  • Sample sizes: The tables I could retrieve show only each group's share of voters, not respondent counts, so nothing here can be re-weighted from the raw sample.
  • Florida only: This is a Florida electorate. It should not be read as a national picture.
  • Not included: A 2026 Florida exit poll was not available when I checked. The 2026 table on Wikipedia is a commented-out placeholder borrowed from another state, so I left it out. I also could not retrieve 2022 or 2024 Florida crosstabs.
  • Small groups can be marked n/a where the pollster judged the sample too small.

Sources to verify

    • 2026 numbers are unofficial election night results from the state, about 99% of expected votes in, and will move until counties certify.
    • Margin is Democratic share minus Republican share of all votes cast in the county. Shift is the 2026 margin minus the comparison margin, in percentage points. Turnout differs a lot between presidential and midterm years, so shifts against 2024 President read differently than shifts against 2022.
    • The governor races add up slightly differently from their statewide totals. The 2022 county rows sum to 4,614,418 for DeSantis and 3,106,328 for Crist, and the 2018 rows sum to 4,075,736 for DeSantis. Check the state's results archive before quoting exact numbers from those two.
